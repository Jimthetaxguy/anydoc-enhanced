## Decision

Strict ODP is implemented as an additive local contract. AnyDoc `0.2.4` remains
pinned; no upstream source was vendored and no dependency update was required.
The public MCP route is enabled only on Linux, where the worker address-space
ceiling is enforceable. PDF routing is unchanged.

## Remaining gates

Darwin has no production process-memory ceiling or filesystem isolation. EPUB
remains containment-only; RTF, HTML/MHTML, OCR, legacy/binary Office formats,
and broader AnyDoc CSV behavior remain unexposed or gated. `cargo-deny` remains
an external CI gate because it is not installed on this host.

## Rollback

provenance entries as one reviewable change. No upstream mirror or dependency
rollback is needed.
